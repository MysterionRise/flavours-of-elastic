## What comes from where

| Column | Source |
|---|---|
| `movieId`, `title`, `genres` | ml-32m `movies.csv`, unchanged (verified by `data/add_ratings.py`) |
| `vote_average`, `vote_count` | computed from ml-32m `ratings.csv` by `data/add_ratings.py`: mean rating × 2 (scale 1–10), rounded half-up to one decimal; `vote_average` is empty below 10 votes. Checksums and policy: `movies_enriched.meta.json` |
| `abstract_*`, `description_*` (en / kk / fr) | **synthetic** — generated by an LLM (`google/gemini-2.0-flash-001` via OpenRouter, February 2026) with `data/generate_descriptions.py` |
| `release_date` (in the index) | synthetic: January 1st of the year in the title |

**The abstracts and descriptions are AI-generated and unreviewed.** The prompt asked for plausible
descriptions, including for films the model did not recognise, so plots, people and facts may be wrong or
invented, and the Kazakh and French texts are machine-generated. Use them as search-engine test data, never
as a factual source about the films.
